FAQ

This is where any prominent questions will be answered.

Q: Will accommodations be provided?
A: We will be renting out a good majority of the living quarters on the ranch. We are offering this dorm-style housing to our lovely guests for free, as long as the space is left cleaner than it was found. The space is first come, first serve. More information is available on our Discord server .

Q: Will there be a Wedding party? (Referring to bridesmaids/groomsmen, etc.)
A: The groom and bride have decided against a wedding party. There will be parents, the officiant, bride, and groom, but nothing else. No guest is more special than another. Everyone was invited for a reason ♡

Q: What's the Child Policy?
A: Children are welcome at the ceremony. Parents will be expected to keep their children from disrupting the ceremony. We don't expect children to be superhumanly quiet, just respectful to the event and others there.

After the post-ceremony photos, we encourage parents to consider taking younger children home in case adult themes may be present. Any remaining children must be under direct supervision of their parents.

Q: Will there be options for people with dietary restrictions?
A: Yes. The RSVP will ask about dietary restrictions and we will do our best to accommodate for these. There may be options outside of your preference/needs but all food should be labelled.

Q: The registry is only funds but I want to buy a gift. Can I?
A: You may contribute to the newlyweds however you wish.

Q: Can I bring a plus-one?
A: We sadly do not have enough space to accommodate more than we already invited. Availability is tight so we spent a lot of time working through our guest list. While filling out the RSVP form, it should tell you who is invited to come along with you.

Just because someone was not invited, doesn't mean we don't appreciate and value them.

Q: I'm not Jewish. What should I know before the wedding?
A: The most important things to keep in mind are about modesty. We require modest attire and a form of head covering for all male presenting people. Head coverings for all will be provided at the event.

Additionally please note that politics are not welcome at the event. Nearly every Jew has some sort of personal connection to the situation in the Middle East and has had to "answer for it". Please keep your opinions on such topics (And politics as a whole) to yourself.

Q: Can I invite someone who did not get an invitation/RSVP?
A: No. We have a very limited capacity so we are keeping our wedding small and intimate. We are sending announcements to many people who we could not fit into our wedding.

Q: Will there be food?
A: There will be food and beverages both during cocktail hour and when dinner is ready.

Q: Is Alcohol allowed?
A: No. This is a dry venue. We hope to create an event that's fun, even without alcohol.

Q: Will there be accommodations for special needs?
A: We encourage any invitees who will need accommodations for special needs to reach out to us via Discord or by using the contact page. Please note that we will be more responsive on Discord.

Q: Will there be a rehearsal dinner?
A: No. We want to keep things simple with one event. Other hangouts may be coordinated as the wedding approaches. Check the Discord server for up to date information on additional events.
