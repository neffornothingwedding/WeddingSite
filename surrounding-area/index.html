Surrounding Area

Rapid City

Home of the airport and third biggest population center, Rapid City doesn’t even qualify as a city. At a population of 80,000 this large town feels like a step into the past of 20 years ago.

Rapid City’s regional airport is the only way in or out of the area by air, so there is a very good chance your South Dakota journey starts here.

If you have dietary needs, it has a food co-op, a Target, two Walmarts, and a Sam’s Club to help you find special marked food for your needs. We will do our best to stock some food in the ranch, but please consider picking up food for yourself.

Food

Everest Cuisine

This little hole-in-the-wall is a favorite of the wedding couple. This is a go-to for date nights with authentic Nepalese food.

Albertanos

This small franchise sells authentic Mexican food. Grab a burrito if that suits your cravings.

Colonial house

This small family-owned restauraunt is known for their pies and soups. They always have fresh (unhectshured) challah on Fridays making it a weeky visit from the wedding couple.

Food Trucks

Check Founder’s Park for food trucks around lunch time. We have a large variety of food trucks with a range of options for meals. (Avoid the pizza truck. We do not recommend)

Places to See

Main Street Square

In historic Downtown Rapid City’s, Main Street Square they host all sorts of events. There’s all sorts of small shops in the walkable area, with something new popping up all the time.

Hotel Alex Johnson

If you want a historic and fancy place to stay, this is it. At the rooftop there is a private restaurant called the Vertex (only accessible to hotel residents) which we have heard a lot of good about. It has a bar, a small menu, and a goregous view of the area. The first two floors are free to roam and show a lot of the local history.

Nearby Towns:

Hill City

Hill City is only a half-hour drive from the ranch: if you want more private accommodations, this is the place to find some.

This is also where the Everspring Inn and Suites are. See Accommodations for more information on our hotel block.

On top of the standard landmarks, check out the local Hill City attractions. We strongly encourage everyone to try the Alpine Inn’s restaurant if you aren’t already planning on it, with delicious German cuisine. Additionally, Hill City offers train rides with a small cafe run out of train cars. If you are exploring beyond the ranch, there is plenty to see in Hill City.

Custer

Custer is also a nearby town of slightly over 30 minutes away. If wild west towns are in your taste, this is a fun place to explore. If you want natural parks and places to explore, there’s nowhere like the area around Custer.

Tourism

South-West of Rapid City (same direction as venue):

Dances With Wolves

If you’re a fan of the classic movie Dances with Wolves, visit the place where it was filmed; It’s just down Mount Rushmore Road.

Reptile Gardens

Reptile Gardens is one of the biggest reptile zoos in the world. They have snake shows and reptile shows for much of the year. There are bird exhibits too for those who are more hesitant towards reptiles.

Rushmore Candy

Right by Reptile Gardens, stop here if you have a sweet tooth or just want to find something weird to eat. There is a lot of variety to the kind of snacks you can come home with.

Crazy Horse

Much bigger than Mount Rushmore, this Work-in-progress monument is here to celebrate the history of the indigenous people who lived here long before us today.

Mount Rushmore

See this carving of the four founding fathers that put South Dakota on the map. This is a classic destination while visiting this side of South Dakota.

Hot Springs

This town has some amazing natural hot springs. Check out Moccasin Springs (adults only) to mellow out and enjoy some peace. If you like paleontology, check out the Mammoth Site where bones found in a back yard uncovered history.

North West of Rapid City:

Deadwood:

Do you like ghost towns? Then this place is great for you. Explore this commercialized piece of history. Note: Deadwood is very heavy in bars and gambling.

Lead: (pronounced leed)

Right by Deadwood, explore a small mining town with super interesting geography.

East of Rapid City:

Badlands:

Explore the beautiful nature in the Badlands. This once-underwater beauty has plenty of hiking trails. This National Park has strong history to the wedding couple.

Wall Drug:

Wall Drug is as “tourist trap” as it gets. Drive on over and follow the sign after sign beconing you in. It may be cheesy, but it’s also charming as heck. If you have plans to bring home an obnoxious souvenir for someone, there is nowhere better than Wall Drug. (Unironically, we highly recommend if you’re going to the Badlands)
