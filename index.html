Our Story:

The groom, Noah Neff, is the eldest son of Adrianne Neff and Carla Grayson. He is from Dexter, Michigan. Noah is currently working towards a job in healthcare as a Physician Assistant. Before getting his Bachelor's of Science in Neuroscience, he spent notable time working in the Conservation Corp and volunteering in medicine.

The bride, Sofia Sadun, is younger daughter of Erica Sadun and Alberto Sadun. She is from Denver, Colorado. She is currently working in Database Administration. She graduated with a Bachelor's of Science in Computer Engineering after years of committing herself to martial arts and cosplay.

Noah and Sofia found each other online during the lockdowns in April 2020. After talking online for a year and a half, they met in person for the fourth of July in Minnesota. Immediately they became official and began their relationship. After nearly 4 years of visiting each other back and forth, they finally moved in together in late Spring 2025. After living together for a while, they agreed to get married. There has not been a proposal yet - maybe one day after the fuss of the wedding is over.

Now they are preparing to adventure into life's next big journey, marriage. Please join us to celebrate the couple and help them enter this new, exciting phase of their life.
