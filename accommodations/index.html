 Cabin Info:

- All spots will available between 4:00 PM on Friday through 10:00 AM on Monday.
- You will need to bring your own supplies to act on these offers.

- Showers and bathrooms will be available to all, but restricted for the season.

- You can not purchase food on campus.

- We will not be able to provide transport from the airport to medicine mountain. **Please consider renting a vehicle or coordinating with someone through our DISCORD SERVER**

If these accommodations do not appeal to you, consider staying in Hill City with our HOTEL BLOCK, less than an hour away from the venue.

Dorm Style Housing (Cabins):

We will have designated male and female dorms as well as a few that can be claimed by (preferrably shared by) family units.

- You will need to bring your own bedding/sleepingbags, toiletries, towels, etc.

- These spots are first come, first serve.

- Check-in is any time after 4:00PM on Friday and checkout will be 10:00 AM on Monday morning.

- These rooms will need to be left cleaner than they were found.

- We will be offering a reward to the cleanest cabin.

Recommended Dorm Packing List:

- luggage
- clothes for the weekend:
  -> nice clothes for wedding
  -> outdoorsy clothes for exploring
  -> swim suit if swimming is desired
- layers for warmth
- waterproof layers
- pillow
- sheet + blanket(s) OR sleeping bag
- toiletries:
  -> toothbrush
  -> toothpaste
  -> shampoo/conditioner/soap
  -> brush
  -> towel
- flipflops/water shoes for the shower
- mess kit (set of dishes and utensils)
- sunscreen
- bug repellant
- food for the weekend (make sure to store safely away from wildlife including mice)

Off Campus Hotel (Hotel Block):

We are working with EverSpring Inn and Suites to offer a courtesy block in Hill City. Hill City is a 30 minute drive from the Ranch and a 30 minute drive from Rapid City.

We have a special deal for guests staying for the Wedding. In order to use this, call in to reserve a room and ask for the discount for the Neff Wedding. If you already made a booking with the hotel, you can call in to get your charge adjusted. In order to get our discount you have to **book before September 25th 2026.** Our rate should knock you down to $99 a night for a 2 queen room. Our deal ends September 25th, 2026.

EverSpring Inn and Suites: +1(605)574-2100

Campsites:

If there is demand, we can also reserve campsites for those who may want to camp out in their own personal space. Please let me know if you want to use one so we rent enough to meet demand.

- We are looking at the Strauss and Adams campsites.

- Tents and camping gear will not be provided.
