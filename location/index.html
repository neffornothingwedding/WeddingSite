About the Weekend Venue

Medicine Mountain Scout Ranch

Address

Medicine Mountain Scout Ranch - BSA
24201 Bobcat Road
Custer, SD 57730

Important Info

Welcome to the Boyscouts of America Medicine Mountain ranch. This is a wilderness camp and will require good camping practice and etiquette.

Make sure to be careful walking around as the ground may be bumpy and unstable. Also remember to leave the place cleaner than you found it and be respectful of the nature and wildlife. Do not feed or approach any animals. Take nothing but pictures and leave nothing but footsteps.

We will be offering Hostel-style accommodations and places to hang out during the weekend for those who might desire. See ACCOMMODATIONS for more info.

For best safety practice please use the buddy system when wandering around the campus to ensure that you can be found and taken care of in the case of an emergency.

Transport

Keep in mind that we are hours from the nearest place that qualifies as a City.
Uber and Lyft will not be accessible so please use our Discord server to communicate about car rentals and rides beforehand.

Parking

General parking is by the entrance during the day of the ceremony and ONLY the day of the ceremony, we will have additional parking available near the ceremony.

Areas

Cabins

We have rented all the cabins on staff hill for those who might wish to stay on campus SEE ACCOMMODATIONS.

Stem Center

The Stem Center is where we plan for people to hang out and socialize. We will be providing some food and snacks to make the stay a little easier but please plan for your own meals to ensure there's enough for everyone.

Nature Lodge

This is another place to hang out if needed. We will not be stocking/monitoring.

Ampitheater

This is where the ceremony will be held barring bad weather. This place has a beautiful view of Lake Alexander and can be a great place to go fishing.

Townley Training Center

This is where the functional bathrooms are.
The center itself will only be available for the reception (post wedding) unless severe weather deems it necessary to move the ceremony indoors.

Activities

This ranch is a wonderful place for various activities if desired. We highly recommend the buddy system for anyone wandering or exploring. We will be using the STEM CENTER as a general communal hub with puzzles and various other options to hang out.

There is also a swimming area in Lake Alexander if you are brave. Make sure to stay within the swimming area as lake Alexander is also a great place to go fishing. There is no life guard on duty so make sure to swim at your own risk.

The area has a beautiful view for nature hikes if you are so inclined. Make sure to bring a buddy and let someone know where you are going.

Surrounding Area

For more info see the Dedicated Page for the Surrounding Area

Hill City

Hill City is only a half-hour drive from the ranch: if you want more private accommodations, this is the place to find some.

On top of the standard landmarks, check out the local Hill City attractions. We strongly encourage everyone to try the Alpine Inn's restaurant if you aren't already planning on it, with delicious German cuisine. Additionally, Hill City offers train rides with a small cafe run out of train cars. If you are exploring beyond the ranch, there is plenty to see in Hill City. 

Custer

Custer is also a nearby town of slightly over 30 minutes. If wild west towns are in your taste, this is a fun place to explore. If you want natural parks and places to explore, there's nowhere like the area around Custer. 

Rapid City

At an hour drive out, Rapid City is the main "city" in the area. You probably landed in the Rapid City Airport if you flew. There is a lot more available in Rapid City. If you have dietary needs, it has a food co-op, a target, two Walmarts, and a Sam's Club to help you find special marked food for your needs. We will do our best to stock some food in the ranch, but please consider picking up food for yourself.
