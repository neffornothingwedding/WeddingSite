Location

Medicine Mountain Scout Ranch

24201 Bobcat Road
Custer, SD 57730

Keep in mind that we are hours from the nearest place that qualifies as a City.
Uber and Lyft will not be accessible so please use our Discord server to communicate about car rentals and rides.

This ranch is a Boy Scouts of America owned ranch that we are renting out for the weekend of the wedding. It is a 30-minute drive from the nearest town, Hill City. Since the space is often used for camps and retreats, we will have access to a variety of activities.

We are renting all of the cabins on the ranch for accommodations. Please check out our FAQ for a brief summary, or the wedding Discord [link] for more info on the living spaces we are renting.

Hill City

Hill City is only a half-hour drive from the ranch: if you want more private accommodations, this is the place to find some.

On top of the standard landmarks, check out the local Hill City attractions. We strongly encourage everyone to try the Alpine Inn's restaurant if you aren't already planning on it, with delicious German cuisine. Additionally, Hill City offers train rides with a small cafe run out of train cars. If you are exploring beyond the ranch, there is plenty to see in Hill City. 

Custer

Custer is also a nearby town of slightly over 30 minutes. If wild west towns are in your taste, this is a fun place to explore. If you want natural parks and places to explore, there's nowhere like the area around Custer. 

Rapid City

At an hour drive out, Rapid City is the main "city" in the area. You probably landed in the Rapid City Airport if you flew. There is a lot more available in Rapid City. If you have dietary needs, it has a food co-op, a target, two Walmarts, and a Sam's Club to help you find special marked food for your needs. We will do our best to stock some food in the ranch, but please consider picking up food for yourself.
