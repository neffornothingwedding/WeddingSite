Wedding Info

Location:

Medicine Mountain Scout Ranch - BSA
24201 Bobcat Road
Custer, SD 57730

Date:

October 11, 2026
4:00PM (Mountain Time)
Please show up around 15-30 minutes early

Attendance:

In order to attend, please fill out the RSVP to make sure you (and your party) are accounted for. See FAQ for more information.

Late guests may be denied entry during the ceremony.

Dress Code:

The location of the event is a wilderness camp meaning you will want to make sure your outfit can handle a short hike from place to place.

That said, please dress modestly. Solid colors are preferred over patterns. Additionally, we request all male presenting people wear yarlmuke/kippah such as the ones we will have provided. We will also provide traditionally female head coverings at the event.

Accommodations:

We will be renting out a good majority of the living quarters on the ranch. We are offering this hostel-style housing to our lovely guests for free, as long as the space is left cleaner than it was found. The space is first come, first serve. More information is available on our Discord.

Child Policy:

Children are welcome at the ceremony. Parents will be expected to keep their children from disrupting the ceremony. We don't expect children to be superhumanly quiet, just respectful to the event and others there.

After the post-ceremony photos, we encourage parents to consider taking younger children home in case adult themes may be present. Any remaining children must be under direct supervision of their parents.

Alcohol Policy:

As per the venue requirements, this will be a dry event. We will do our best to keep it enjoyable in the absence
.
