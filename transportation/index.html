Transportation

Please keep in mind when scheduling that Rapid City does not have much access to Uber and Lyft; availability is minimal. We recommended that each family unit rent/bring a car or coordinate to ride with someone who will.

The wedding will be an hour drive from Rapid City, which is the nearest airport. We HEAVILY encourage finding a car rental. 

Parking:

General Parking 

For those who intend to stay over the weekend or those without mobility needs, we will have general parking at the ranch entrance (0.6 miles/1km from the ceremony)

Special Event Parking 

During the event we will have LIMITED parking next to the ceremony during the day-of only. Please leave these spots for those with mobility needs.

We will also have a transport vehicle for those who will need a ride to the reception (0.3 miles/0.5 km from the ceremony)
